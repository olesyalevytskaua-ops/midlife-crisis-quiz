Behind the plot twist

Feelings first. Hobbies second.

Twelve questions explore six themes: energy, curiosity, connection, care, structure and independence. Each answer contributes equally. We centre and normalise the six themes, then compare your profile with 17 hand-written archetype profiles and select the closest one. This keeps moderate profiles from dominating simply because they sit near the scoring centre.

If two profiles are equally close, we compare how many individual answers match their reference responses, then the “next chapter” answer, then the archetype’s alphabetical identifier. The same answers always give the same result.

This is a playful, unvalidated reflection tool. It does not diagnose a crisis, measure your personality or predict what you should do. You may recognise yourself in several results. No answers are sent to a server or included in shared links.
